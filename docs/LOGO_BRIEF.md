# Hardware One — Logo Brief

## The name
**Hardware One**

## One-line essence
A modular firmware that turns a scattered collection of cheap microcontrollers
into a single, unified system.

## The core idea to convey
**Many individual pieces forming something greater than the sum of its parts.**

The whole project is built on a private, router-free **mesh** (each device talks
directly to the others over a custom ESP-NOW protocol). Separate little devices —
each doing its own job — pool their abilities and act as *one* system. That idea of
**unity emerging from independent nodes** is what the logo should capture. The "One"
in the name is the point: scattered parts, one system.

## Tone / personality
Technical but approachable. Clean and modern, not corporate-sterile. It should feel
like something a maker/hacker is proud to put on a device. A small amount of
personality is welcome; it doesn't need to be cold or strictly geometric.

## Color
**Minimal / monochrome.** Primarily black and white, with at most one restrained
accent color. Clean, hacker-friendly, and ages well. (This also makes the
single-color requirement below easy to satisfy.)

## Direction ideas (inspiration, not requirements — surprise me)
- **Mesh / network**: nodes connected by lines forming a shape, constellation, or
  cohesive silhouette — individual dots resolving into one form.
- **Modular pieces**: distinct blocks/tiles/hexagons assembling into a whole; a
  unit built from smaller units.
- **The "One"**: the numeral 1, or the word "One," constructed *out of* smaller
  nodes/parts rather than drawn as a solid shape.
- **Optional mascot**: a small, friendly hardware/robot character is acceptable if
  it fits — but it is **not** required, and I'd prefer the mesh/unity concept lead.

## Must work as
- A **square app icon** (it's used in a web dashboard and on devices).
- **Single-color** version (one flat color on a transparent background) in addition
  to full color — it needs to read when small and when monochrome.
- Legible at **small sizes** (think favicon / 32px) — avoid fine detail that
  disappears when shrunk.
- Ideally a **logomark** (icon alone) plus a **lockup** (icon + "Hardware One"
  wordmark).

## Please avoid
- Generic "smart home" clichés (houses, WiFi fan symbols).
- Overly busy circuit-board textures.
- Stock-looking AI-generated gradients/blobs.

## Deliverables I'd like
- Vector source (SVG or AI) plus exported PNGs.
- Color + single-color (monochrome) variants.
- Logomark on its own and the full lockup with the wordmark.

## Background / about the project
See the attached **Quick Explanation** for the full picture of what Hardware One
does. Short version: it's an operating-system-like firmware for ESP32 chips; you
flash each chip for a specific role (sensor, camera node, smart-glasses companion,
mesh relay, etc.), and they all speak the same mesh protocol so they work together
as one system you control from a single dashboard.
